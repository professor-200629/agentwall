Agent Wall

An AI agent plans one task on a board on your real wall. Nothing goes out until you pinch and hold to approve.

  1. Ask: point at a chip and pinch (thumb + index finger), or pinch 🎤 Speak.
  2. Change: pinch “Only after 4 PM”, or an “Also free” time. The invite rewrites itself.
  3. Approve: pinch and hold the invite for 1 second. It goes to a demo outbox. Nothing is really sent.
  4. More: pinch “Focus 2 h”, or pinch one of your meetings on the week to move it.

Approved by you

    Use my own calendar (optional)

    Your calendar is read in this browser. Only busy times and short titles of the next two working weeks are kept, in this browser. A link is fetched through Agent Wall's small server, because calendars do not let a web page read them directly; the server keeps nothing. The AI gets free times and the guest's name, never your event titles. Agent Wall never sends anything.
