Agent Wall

An AI agent plans your week on your real wall; the one card that needs you lands on your desk. When a time you approved stops fitting, the wall blocks it, shows why, and lets you try another. Nothing leaves without your pinch-and-hold. It starts on a labelled sample week.

What to do
  1. Try it: point at “Try it” on the wall and pinch (thumb + index finger). A labelled example puts a new busy block over an approved call.
  2. Choose: pinch one of the two big times. It is only a preview: the old time stays on the wall, marked CONFLICT.
  3. Approve: pinch and hold the card on your desk for 1 second. The old invite is blocked; the new one waits here as an email draft and a calendar file.
  4. Move things: pinch the bar on top of the board, or the bar under the card, and move your hand. Let the board go near a wall and it snaps on.
  5. Also: pinch “Find time + draft invite” to plan from scratch, or 🎤 Speak a limit such as “not before lunch”.

    Use my own calendar (optional)

    Your calendar is read in this browser. Only busy times and short titles of the next two working weeks are kept, in this browser. A link is fetched through Agent Wall's small server, because calendars do not let a web page read them directly; the server keeps nothing. The AI gets free times and the guest's name, never your event titles. Agent Wall never sends anything.
